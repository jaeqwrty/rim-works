## 📖 About

**RimWorks PH** is a full-stack web application that streamlines the custom rim ordering experience for Filipino vehicle owners. Customers can browse a curated catalog of rims, pick their preferred finish and design, place an order, and upload their downpayment proof — all in one place.

Admins get a dedicated staff portal to manage orders, verify payments, and keep operations running smoothly.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛒 Customer Side
- Browse rim products with before & after photos
- Filter by vehicle type, finish, color, and design
- Place orders with a full configuration snapshot
- Upload downpayment proof from any device
- Track order status in real time

### 🛠️ Admin Side
- Secure staff portal with role-based access control
- View and manage all customer orders
- Verify downpayment submissions
- Update order statuses with full audit history
- Manage the entire rim product catalog
